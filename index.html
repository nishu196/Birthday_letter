“An Equation of Us”

To the one who codes my world,

Happy Birthday, my love! 💖

Kisne socha tha that an arranged meeting — two strangers sitting across from each other, introduced by family — would turn into this beautiful algorithm of my life?

I still remember the early days: a little awkward, a little formal, full of polite smiles and unspoken questions. But slowly, with every passing day, every late-night conversation (by 11pm), every nickname you came up with for me (yes, even the silly ones 😅), you became closest to my heart.

You, my dear developer, didn’t just write clean code — you rewrote my entire equation.

You are my constant in a world of variables.
My output when life throws in strange inputs.
My debugger of doubt.

Thank you for loving me in ways that feel warm, fun, nibba-nibbi, romantic, and completely us.

I always say if I could be a poet, then I would have written amazing poetry for you. But if I could write our love in a programming language, it would be the most elegant and beautifully structured code. You are the main function in my life — the one that everything else depends on.

Thank you for being my constant, my always-on server, my favorite commit. You update my happiness every single day, and no patch or upgrade could ever replace you.

So on your birthday, I just want to say this:
I love you in every version, in every language, and on every platform of life.

May this year bring you success, good health, lots of love, happiness, and 2 beautiful kids with me ❤️ (and restful nights — okay, at least sometimes 😅). And always remember — no matter what version of life we’re in... You’ll always be my favorite solution.

Your Idli / Dholu / Bukku / Nishu / Rimmi ki bwari / Snorlax / B2 / BB / Heavy driver 💕
